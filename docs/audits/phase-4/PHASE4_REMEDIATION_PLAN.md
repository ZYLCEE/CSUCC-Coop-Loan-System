## Readiness gates

1. Produce a redacted deployment inventory covering the portal revision, Edge Function revision, migration revisions, effective DB grants and RLS, private storage policies and job names.
2. Close P4-01, P4-02 and P4-03 for the intended target, verify credential rotation and complete a full approved dependency scan.
3. Verify the release and transition controls, test both roles and both account isolation cases, and reconcile synthetic financial results.
4. Record isolated UAT evidence and obtain system-owner and finance/security sign-off. Use production only for a separately authorized rollout and approved read-only smoke checks.
